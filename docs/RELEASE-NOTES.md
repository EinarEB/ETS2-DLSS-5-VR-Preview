# ETS2 DLSS 5 VR Preview v0.1

DLSS 5 neural rendering for Euro Truck Simulator 2 in VR, with Snowymoon lighting, four quality presets and separate settings.

**Download the Windows ZIP below**, extract it, and open **Read me first.html**. It links the five required third-party files. Put them in **Required files**, then run **Setup ETS2 VR Preview.exe**. Setup validates your PC and files before installation.

Requires Windows 11 x64, RTX 50-series with at least 16 GB VRAM, NVIDIA driver 616.64 or newer, ETS2 VR 1.60.1.1007, OpenXR and Snowymoon access. Start with **Medium · Natural · Cooler · intensity 2**. Scroll Lock compares the neural effect; Home opens live controls.

Experimental preview: flicker, distortion and compatibility issues remain possible. Third-party model, consumer, mod and game files are supplied separately. The Windows executables are not code-signed.
