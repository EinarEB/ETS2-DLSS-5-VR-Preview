What these images show

Real input and output from the same captured VR frame. Each view shows a full left-eye image at its original proportions. Full-resolution lossless images are available above so you can inspect fine detail.

The comparison is captured before optional ReShade color grading. The original already includes Snowymoon lighting; the slider isolates the neural edit.

About the preview

Flicker and distortion can still occur in motion. Still screenshots show the appearance of the effect; they cannot show headset comfort or performance.

Medium comparisons are available now. Low, High and Ultra comparisons are coming.
